## Summary

Two bug fixes in this session:

### 1. Daily Roll Call mis-categorized in Student Detail Attendance Tab

**Bug**: When a teacher logged a daily roll call, the record appeared under the subject section (e.g. "Science Year 7") instead of the "Daily Roll Call" section.

**Root causes**:
- `FormTeacherPage` picked a subject course (e.g. Science Year 7) via `myCourses.find(c => c.gradeLevel === ...)` because the DAILY001 roll call course was not in the teacher's assigned courses. The topic was set correctly but the courseId was wrong.
- `StudentDetailPage` `isRollCall` filter used only `topic.startsWith('Daily Roll Call')`. Seeded records had topic "Daily Morning Roll Call" which does NOT start with "Daily Roll Call" — falling through to subject section.

**Fix**:
- Added `POST /attendance/roll-call` backend endpoint that always looks up the `DAILY001` course automatically, so the frontend never needs to pass a courseId.
- Simplified `FormTeacherPage.rollCallMutation` to call the new endpoint; removed unused `myCourses` query.
- Strengthened `StudentDetailPage` `isRollCall` to check `session.course?.code === 'DAILY001'` (primary) OR topic prefix (fallback).
- Fixed seed topic from `"Daily Morning Roll Call"` → `"Daily Roll Call — School Wide"` for consistency.

### 2. Grade Management shows mostly empty rows

**Bug**: In Grade Management, selecting a course showed the majority of students with empty grade columns.

**Root cause**: 28 Year 7A extra students were enrolled in MATH701 and SCI701 but had zero grade records. Year 8 courses had no grade items and no enrolled students at all.

**Fix**:
- Added bulk grade seeding for all 28 `y7aExtraIds` in MATH701 and SCI701. Performance distribution: A(20%) B(30%) C(30%) D(15%) F(5%), deterministic jitter per student index. Final Exam (future due date) left un-graded intentionally.
- Added `makeGradeItems` calls for all 5 Year 8 courses (Math, English, Science, MIB, ICT).
- Enrolled ~60 Year 8A/8B students in their courses and seeded grade data for them.
